# Tags used in applies_when of financial.csv (comma-separated). Style follows ALL, LISTED, PUBLIC_CO.
ALL: every company (not used in financial.csv)
LISTED: entity listed on a recognised stock exchange
PUBLIC_CO: public company
BANK_COMM: commercial banks
BANK_SFB: small finance banks
BANK_PAY: payments banks
BANK_COOP: urban/state/central co-operative banks
BANK_RRB: regional rural banks
NBFC: non-banking financial companies (incl. NBFC-AA, NBFC-MFI where relevant)
HFC: housing finance companies
ARC: asset reconstruction companies
MFI: microfinance lenders
DIGITAL_LENDING: REs and LSPs in digital lending
INS_LIFE: life insurers
INS_GEN: general/health insurers
INS_REINS: reinsurers
INS_BROKER: insurance brokers
TPA: third party administrators
SEC_BROKER: stock brokers
SEC_DP: depository participants
SEC_RTA: registrars and transfer agents
SEC_MB: merchant bankers
SEC_AMC: AMCs/mutual funds
SEC_AIF: alternative investment funds
SEC_PMS: portfolio managers
SEC_INVIT: infrastructure investment trusts
SEC_REIT: real estate investment trusts
SEC_CRA: credit rating agencies
SEC_IA: investment advisers
SEC_RA: research analysts
SEC_MII: stock exchanges, depositories, clearing corporations
PAY_PA: payment aggregators/gateways
PAY_PPI: prepaid payment instrument issuers
PAY_TREDS: TReDS platforms
PAY_AA: account aggregators
PMLA_RE: reporting entities under PMLA
FATCA_RE: reporting financial institutions (FATCA/CRS)
PENSION: PFRDA-regulated entities (NPS)
IFSC: IFSCA-regulated units in GIFT City
CHIT: chit fund foremen
NIDHI: Nidhi companies
FOREX_AD: authorised dealers (AD-I/II/III)
FOREX_FFMC: full fledged money changers
